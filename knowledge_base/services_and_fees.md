# Real Estate Knowledge Base — Services & Fees

## Stanfles Realty

Stanfles Realty describes a 100% commission brokerage model.

Publicly stated fee information includes:

- $594 per transaction for files under $1 million.
- $1,000 per $1 million for transactions above $1 million.
- E&O is included in the stated transaction structure.
- No monthly fees.
- No franchise fees.
- No desk fees.
- No technology fees.
- Annual E&O of $99 is stated for inactive agents.
- Stanfles also provides escrow, loans, transaction coordination and remote online notary services.

The website states that commissions are paid at escrow.

## Partner Real Estate

Partner Real Estate provides transaction coordination support.

For Partner Real Estate team agents:

- Transaction Coordinator service is included at no additional cost.

For National Network Partner Program / LPT Realty agents:

- Transaction Coordinator service is stated as $500 per closing, paid at close of escrow.
- No monthly fees.
- No annual fees.

Transaction coordination includes:

- Document uploading
- Compliance coordination
- Communication with escrow and vendors
- Closing support
- Deadline tracking
- Contract addenda management
- Signature collection
- File auditing
- Commission breakdown preparation
- Digital file delivery at closing

Partner Real Estate also promotes a Pre-Listing Concierge Service that manages repairs and strategic renovations before listing without upfront costs, according to its public website.

## REAL New York

REAL New York's renter information states:

- No fee is charged for information provided by the website.
- No fee is charged for viewing apartments or rental properties through its brokers.
- A broker fee is charged if a client rents a property through REAL New York, except where the property is designated NO FEE.
- For unfurnished apartments with a 1–2 year lease, the stated broker fee is usually 15% of annual rent.
- Furnished/short-term rental pricing should be confirmed directly with REAL New York.

REAL New York also publishes transaction-related fees for specific properties and transaction types. These may include application fees, security deposits, transfer taxes, attorney fees and other property-specific charges.
