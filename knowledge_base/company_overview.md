# Real Estate Knowledge Base — Company Overview

## Stanfles Realty

Stanfles Realty describes itself as a 100% commission real estate brokerage serving Los Angeles and Orange County. Its public website highlights premium office locations, real people and broker support, payment at escrow, and related in-house services.

Stanfles also provides escrow, discounted loans, transaction coordination and remote online notary services.

The company website lists offices/services associated with Los Angeles, Orange County and nearby Southern California locations.

## Zown

Zown is a Canadian real estate company focused on home buying and selling in Ontario.

Zown promotes a full-service home-selling model and provides support including professional photography, staging advice, open houses, MLS exposure, pricing, marketing, negotiation and closing support.

Zown also provides access to a network of active buyers across Ontario.

## REAL New York

REAL New York is a New York real estate company providing rental and real estate services.

Its website provides rental guides, seller resources, property listings, broker services and information about rental and transaction-related fees.

REAL New York's public resources include information for renters, sellers and purchasers.
